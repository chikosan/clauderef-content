Anthropic released **Claude Opus 4.7** for complex software engineering and professional work.

## What changed

- Stronger performance on difficult coding tasks that require sustained reasoning.
- More consistent instruction following and greater willingness to verify work before declaring success.
- Higher-resolution image understanding and better visual output for interfaces, slides, and documents.
- Claude Code support for `xhigh` effort and eligible auto-mode workflows arrived with the model.

## Why it matters

Opus 4.7 was aimed at tasks users previously needed to supervise closely. Verification behavior helps, but passing tests and reviewing the resulting diff remain the reliable completion standard.
