Anthropic released **Claude Sonnet 5** as a more agentic successor to Sonnet 4.6.

## What changed

- Stronger planning, tool use, coding, knowledge work, and autonomous execution.
- Availability across Claude plans, Claude Code, and the Claude Platform.
- Introductory API pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through August 31, followed by standard Sonnet pricing.
- An updated tokenizer that can map the same input to roughly 1.0–1.35 times as many tokens, depending on content.
- New cyber safeguards and lower measured rates of several undesirable behaviors than Sonnet 4.6.

## Why it matters

Sonnet 5 narrows the gap between lower-cost Sonnet workflows and Opus-level agent tasks. Recheck token budgets during migration because tokenizer changes can alter usage even when prompts stay identical.
