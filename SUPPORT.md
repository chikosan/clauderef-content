# Support

ClaudeREF is community-maintained and cannot provide official Anthropic product support.

- Search [ClaudeREF.com](https://clauderef.com) and existing issues first.
- Use repository discussions for content questions and ideas.
- Use the issue forms for factual corrections and ecosystem submissions.
- Use Anthropic's official support channels for accounts, billing, availability, and authoritative
  product support.
- Report vulnerabilities privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Do not include credentials, private prompts, customer data, or other sensitive information in an
issue, discussion, screenshot, or reproduction.
