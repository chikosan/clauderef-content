# Community code of conduct

ClaudeREF is a technical community where people should be able to contribute, ask questions, and
disagree without hostility.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing the work rather than the person.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Cite sources, correct mistakes openly, and distinguish evidence from opinion.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
spam, impersonation, and publishing private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project communication, and
public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@clauderef.com](mailto:hello@clauderef.com). Include only
the information needed to understand the incident. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly
as practical; retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
